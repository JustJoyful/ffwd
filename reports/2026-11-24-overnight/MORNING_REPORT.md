So it is **not** the KV type, **not** the weight quantisation and **not** the Mesa shader cache. Speed varied between 5 and 27 tok/s for the same configuration without a pattern I could find; correctness did not vary. The same Q5_K_M model scores 96 to 97/100 on the NVIDIA adapter, so the model and prompts are fine: this points at the WebGPU kernels on this driver and browser build (not investigated further: other Intel drivers, Chrome stable, Firefox untested). The very first prompt of every iGPU run took 40 to 70 s (one-off shader compilation); warm turns were about 2.5 s but the text was wrong.
- **Verdict against the criteria on this simulated budget laptop with WebGPU: FAIL on parity (0 of 4 genuine tasks) and effectively on median turn (133 s in the run; the 2.5 s warm turns in the probes carry wrong text, so they mean nothing); tab responsiveness PASSES**: F4 re-encode in ffmpeg.wasm took 21.0 s alone and 21.0 s while the model generated, longest main-thread stall 3.3 ms (idle 0.7 ms), no slowdown measurable (the model's "alone" rate of 7.7 tok/s includes the one-off shader compile, 26.8 tok/s together). Load times from localhost: cold 2.4 s, warm 1.6 s, after a browser restart 2.2 s.
- **What this means (recommendation, not built):** WebGPU must not be trusted without a check. At model load run a tiny canary prompt with a known expected reply, fall back to the processor path on a mismatch, and keep showing which path is in use. The bench kit would also have caught this (its "agreement with the native reply" number would have been about 0). A real budget laptop and a few other iGPUs are the launch checklist's next evidence; this is one adapter on one driver.
- **CPU: thread choice matters a lot.** Pinned to 4 P-core threads it ran at 36 tok/s (3.2 s median); my first CPU run (wllama's default 6 threads on this 12-thread hybrid CPU, with other load around) gave 17 tok/s and 6.8 s. The measured waits in the app's CPU notice (10 s first step, 6 s later) came from that slower run, so they are pessimistic for a well-pinned run; they were left unchanged until more machines are measured. A thread default such as min(4, cores/2) is worth testing.
